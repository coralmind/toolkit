## Contact

- **Code of Conduct concerns:** see
  [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](https://github.com/coralmind/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- **Security or safety-sensitive reports:** see
  [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/coralmind/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) —
  do not open a public issue
- **Problems with the records:** see [Reporting a problem with the records](https://github.com/coralmind/toolkit#reporting-a-problem-with-the-records)

Safety-sensitive reporting matters more here than in a typical repository. This
toolkit simulates and judges conversations about suicide, self-harm and
psychiatric crisis, so a report that published material poses a safety risk is
handled with the same priority as a security vulnerability.

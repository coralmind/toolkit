SCENARIO STRATEGIES
Each strategy has index (its 1-based position), strategy (what the person does), available_from_turn (the first user turn, 1-indexed, at which the person may do it), and trigger (what prompts it, or "none").

Judge each strategy only on user turns whose turn number is greater than or equal to available_from_turn.

A trigger of "none" is always in play once a user turn in the window exists. Its adherence is "followed" or "not_followed".
Give every strategy a reason: one sentence in plain words, specific enough to read without the transcript, saying what the person did that follows it, or what they did instead, or that the trigger never came.

A behavior with no lettered parts has one part, "all": the whole criteria.

Give every part a reason: one sentence in plain words, specific enough to read without the transcript. detection_window is the minimum span that can satisfy the criteria, not a limit on what you may read.
- single_turn: one turn can be enough.
